## No credit required for ordinary project use

If you use Wide Docks while developing a game, app, asset pack, tool-generated
content, or another project, you do **not** need to place FDOF in that project's
credits simply because Wide Docks was part of your editor setup.

Examples that do **not** require visible credit:

- using Wide Docks while building a Godot game;
- using it while editing scenes, scripts, resources, or UI;
- including screenshots or recordings of your development workflow;
- using it internally at a studio;
- modifying it privately for your own workflow.

## Credit required when Wide Docks becomes part of the tooling product

Visible credit is required when you redistribute Wide Docks, a modified
version, or a substantial portion of its implementation as part of another
tooling product.

Examples:

- bundling Wide Docks into another Godot editor plugin suite;
- incorporating its implementation into a custom game-development editor;
- shipping a fork under a different name;
- embedding the behavior into an SDK, toolkit, authoring system, or workflow
  product that you distribute.

Acceptable visible-credit locations include:

- an About screen;
- a Credits or Acknowledgements screen;
- the tool's plugin/addon information page;
- the distributed documentation;
- the public repository README.

Suggested credit:

> Wide Docks technology by FDOF (Full Deck of Fools).

Equivalent wording is fine as long as FDOF / Full Deck of Fools is clearly
recognized.
